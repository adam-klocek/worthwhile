# Bundled font assets

Inter and Lora Latin WOFF2 files (regular 400 and semibold 600) are distributed from `@fontsource/inter` 5.3.0 and `@fontsource/lora` 5.3.0. Their respective SIL Open Font License texts and copyright notices are included in this folder. Worthwhile serves these assets locally; it makes no font-service request.
